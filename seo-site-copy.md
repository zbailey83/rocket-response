## [FAQ SECTION]

# Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q: Will my customers know they're talking to AI?**
A: Most won't. Our AI voice agent uses advanced natural language processing and sounds remarkably human. For text-based conversations, the AI communicates in a natural, conversational tone customized to your brand. However, we always recommend transparency — and we can configure your agent to disclose that it's an AI assistant if you prefer or if required in your industry.

**Q: How long does setup take?**
A: Most businesses are fully live within 48–72 hours. Our team handles everything — we train your AI agent, connect your channels, integrate your tools, and test everything before launch. You'll have a kickoff call, review the AI's responses, and approve before going live.

**Q: What happens if the AI can't handle a question or situation?**
A: Rocket Response AI is smart enough to know what it doesn't know. When a conversation requires human intervention — a complex technical question, an upset customer, a unique situation — the AI seamlessly escalates to you or your team via instant notification (text, email, app, or Slack). You can jump into any conversation at any time.

**Q: Can I customize what the AI says?**
A: Absolutely. During onboarding, we work with you to define your AI agent's personality, tone, approved responses, pricing guidelines, qualification criteria, and more. You have full control over what your AI agent says and doesn't say. And you can request changes anytime.

**Q: Do I need any technical skills?**
A: Zero. We handle all the technical setup, integration, and training. If you can answer questions about your business in a 15-minute call, you have all the "technical skills" required.

**Q: What if I already have a CRM or scheduling tool?**
A: Great — we integrate with it. We work with ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber, GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Salesforce, Google Calendar, Calendly, and hundreds of other tools via direct integration or Zapier.

**Q: Is there really no contract?**
A: Correct. Month-to-month billing. Cancel anytime with no penalties or fees. We keep customers by delivering results, not by locking you in. We also offer a 30-day money-back guarantee — if you're not satisfied in your first 30 days, we'll refund every penny.

**Q: Can the AI handle multiple languages?**
A: Yes. Our standard agent operates in English. Spanish language support is available as an add-on ($99/month). Additional languages are available on Enterprise plans.

**Q: Is this HIPAA compliant?**
A: Yes. For healthcare businesses (dental, chiropractic, med spa, veterinary, mental health, etc.), Rocket Response AI is configured with HIPAA-compliant messaging and data handling protocols included on all plans at no extra cost.

**Q: What if I want to keep my existing phone number?**
A: No problem. We can port your existing business number or set up call forwarding so your AI agent answers when you can't. Your customers won't notice any change.

**Q: How is a "conversation" counted?**
A: One conversation = one complete interaction with one lead, regardless of how many messages are exchanged within that interaction. A 15-message text thread with one lead = 1 conversation. A phone call with one lead = 1 conversation. We don't nickel-and-dime you on individual messages.

**Q: Can I see what the AI is saying to my leads?**
A: Yes — every conversation is logged in your real-time dashboard. Full transcripts, recordings (for voice calls), timestamps, lead details, booking confirmations, and outcomes. Complete transparency, always.
